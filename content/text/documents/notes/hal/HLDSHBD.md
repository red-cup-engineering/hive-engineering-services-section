LDSHBD is a liquid token derived from pooled staked HBD. The token is managed by a smart contract that stakes all deposited HBD, earning rewards that are automatically compounded back into the pool. HLDSHBD represents a proportional claim on the pool, including both the staked principal and accrued rewards. The token can be traded or integrated into DeFi protocols, offering holders liquidity and yield simultaneously. Redemption is facilitated by sending HLDSHBD to the contract, which unstakes and returns the corresponding share of the pool’s value to the sender.H
